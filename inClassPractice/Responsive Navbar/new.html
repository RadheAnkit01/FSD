About BSS

A Legacy of Protection (Est. 1997)


Founded by Mr. J. K. Bishwas, Bishwas Security Services (BSS) is a professionally managed private security and manpower provider headquartered in New Delhi. With over two decades of excellence, we specialize in delivering trained personnel, strict supervision, quick response management, and centralized monitoring.


We pride ourselves on maintaining the highest standards of safety, discipline, and reliability for our diverse portfolio of clients across the Delhi NCR region.
